# Gym Member & Trainer User Guide

---

## 1. Quick Start Flow

1. **Sign In:**
   - Tap **Fast Touch PIN** and enter your 4-6 digit member PIN, or sign in with your email/password.
2. **Choose Category:**
   - Tap **Gym Training** for dynamic rep counting (Push-ups, Bench press, Bicep curls, Lateral raises, Sitting posture).
   - Tap **Yoga Postures** for alignment and hold coaching (Tadasana, Tree Pose, Triangle, Warrior II, Chair).
3. **Select Exercise:**
   - Browse the catalog, check the target muscle groups, and tap **Start**.
4. **Camera Positioning & Calibration:**
   - Review the camera placement cue ("Step back until full body is in frame" or "Turn sideways for side profile").
   - Hold still during the 3-2-1 countdown while the system calibrates your personal limb lengths.
5. **Live Workout & Ghost Skeleton:**
   - Move naturally.
   - Watch the **Dashed Ghost Skeleton** to see the ideal reference form.
   - Watch your own skeleton: green lines indicate correct form; amber indicates minor deviations; red indicates corrections needed.
   - Follow real-time spoken cues and on-screen angle meters.
6. **Session Summary:**
   - Tap **Complete Workout**.
   - Review your total reps, average form score, stability trends, and actionable AI coach tips.
